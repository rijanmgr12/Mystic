About Us

We are passionate about Nepal and its unmatched beauty.

Our Story

Most travel websites promote the same few places: Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan and the Everest region. Many beautiful and culturally rich destinations get little attention online, and travellers have to search across many sites to plan a trip.


Mystic Nepal brings hidden and popular destinations together in one place. You can search and filter locations, read practical tips, and estimate your travel budget in a few clicks.

Our Mission

Promote lesser-known destinations and support sustainable tourism in Nepal.

Our Vision

Make Nepal a top destination for travellers who want authentic, responsible experiences.

Our Values

Authenticity, sustainability, responsibility and community.
